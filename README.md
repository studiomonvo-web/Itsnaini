Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step. Bodoni Moda and DM Sans are bundled locally in `assets/fonts`.

## Fill in before you share the link
Open `index.html`, search for `const PROFILE` near the bottom, and fill in `email` and `linkedin`.
The contact buttons appear automatically once they are filled in.

## Case studies
The Career Journey cards summarise the progression from education sales (2019–2020), to Training & Development (2020–2023), programme coordination (2023–present), and Learning Data & Insights work (2026–present). Each card links to its detailed case study below.

Open `index.html` and search for `CASE_STUDIES` to update the five case studies: consultative education sales, sales capability development, CRM and audience insights, learning operations, and evaluation reporting. Each entry is independent; add, reorder or update entries there and the layout follows. The CRM case includes a five-stage customer engagement workflow and two public-course campaign examples. Keep metrics and personal responsibilities aligned with the source records.

## Animation (new)
- Reveals, bloom and parallax are plain CSS plus a small IntersectionObserver. Nothing is added when "reduce motion" is on.
- Botanical artwork is in `assets/flora/` (soft-focus branches, generated artwork). Swap in your own cherry blossom photography with the same file names and transparent backgrounds.
